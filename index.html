About

Dr. Amritpal Singh is an Assistant Professor (Grade-I) in the Department of Computer Science & Engineering at Dr B R Ambedkar National Institute of Technology (NIT) Jalandhar, where he has served since November 2019. His research centres on machine learning, big data analytics and memory-efficient algorithms, with particular expertise in probabilistic data structures and their applications in healthcare, smart cities and the Internet of Things.

His current work extends to federated learning and large language models, including reliable LLM-based agents and practical AI tools such as institutional chatbots and academic support systems. He earned his Ph.D. from Thapar Institute of Engineering and Technology, Patiala (2018), and brings more than eight years of teaching and research experience to the classroom and the laboratory.

He has authored 28 journal papers, contributed to books from CRC Press, Springer and Wiley, led sponsored and institutional research projects, and supervised doctoral, postgraduate and undergraduate research. He has been listed among the world's Top 2% scientists in the Stanford University–Elsevier ranking (2022 and 2025).
